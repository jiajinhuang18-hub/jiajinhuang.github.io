Multi-Period AC Optimal Power Flow with Grid-Interactive Data Center Workload Flexibility

This page provides the simulation parameters and numerical results associated with the paper.

Simulation Setup

  • Test system: Modified IEEE 118-bus system
  • System base: 100 MVA
  • Data centers: 5
  • Scheduling horizon: 6 periods
  • Time resolution: 10 minutes
  • Maximum workload delay: 3 periods (30 minutes)
